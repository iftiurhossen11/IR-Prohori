CYBER LAW SUMMARY FOR STUDENTS IN BANGLADESH

The Cyber Security Act 2023 protects people from online harm.

What is illegal:
1. Sending abusive or threatening messages online.
2. Sharing someone's private photos without consent.
3. Creating fake profiles to harm someone.
4. Spreading hate speech against any religion, gender, or community.
5. Publishing false information that harms a person's reputation.

What you can do:
1. File a complaint at the nearest police station.
2. Contact the Cyber Crime Unit.
3. Use the National Emergency Helpline 999.
4. Keep evidence: screenshots, links, dates, and times.
