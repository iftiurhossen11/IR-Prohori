ONLINE SAFETY GUIDE FOR STUDENTS

If someone is harassing you online:

1. Do not reply to the harasser.
2. Take screenshots of all messages and posts.
3. Block the person on the platform.
4. Report the account to the platform (Facebook, TikTok, Instagram).
5. Save the evidence in a safe folder.
6. Tell a trusted friend, teacher, or family member.
7. Contact the National Helpline 999 if you feel unsafe.
8. For cybercrime, contact the Cyber Crime Unit of Bangladesh Police.

HELPLINE NUMBERS:
- National Emergency: 999
- Cyber Crime Unit: 01320001000
- Women and Children Helpline: 109
